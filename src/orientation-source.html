Outpatient OR case booking workflow 

  • MD creates case request, then puts in case info into OR calendar into OR block day in Mircosoft Teams (WCR or DRV), additional info can be placed in comments cell on calendar (ie last date which procedure can be done, special requests).

  • Let the patient know when doing appointment the date you’re slotting them in for so they are aware and free for the day. 

  • Scheduling staff will contact patient and confirm they can make assigned date, give them preliminary instructions. 

  • 2-3 weeks prior to OR block day, manager will email schedule of confirmed patients to OR scheduler. 

  • MD who does OR case will notify the requesting MD after the case for follow up.  

  • Any last minute cancellations- OR scheduler notifies manager and manager attempts to fill with any pending urgent cases- if reconfirmed then manager notifies OR scheduler of change

  1. What can MA’s help me with?

  1. Room clinic patients (including virtual rooming for VCON’s). MDP’s and med rec should be completed by them before the visit. They will communicate with you via Teams about a patients readiness and should reach out to you by 8:45am or 1:45pm to let you know which MA will be rooming your patients.

  2. Help perform administrative tasks and followup with patients as directed by the MD.

    • You can send these requests/ tasks as a staff message or ancillary order encounter to your assigned MA Inbox (i.e. p DRV GI MA, or p WCR GI MA)

  3. Schedule all followup clinic appointments. Be sure to specify type of visit [VA15, VA30, TA15, TA30] and timeframe (for ex, within 2-3 weeks). Consider using this dot phrase: .CGMAFOLLOWUP2

  • There is “ITS metrics” that need to be met, which limits conversion of new appt slots to followups. Currently all conversions are on hold unless urgent (these can be approved by Ahilan Arulanandan).

Appt conversion rules:

  • VCON (new) to TAV/ VAV followup conversion okay for:

  • Post-hospital followup

  • Second opinion

  • S/p direct book procedure, needs clinic followup

  • When you send your message to the MA to schedule the appt, please write “okay to convert VCON to TAV or VAV, patient is new to me.”

Clinic Appointment Booking Timeframes

            Return visits:

  • Okay to book VAV/ TAV followup appointments THE AFTERNOON PRIOR if unused VCON slots available on MD schedule the next day (would require appt conversion).

  • Okay to book VAV/ TAV followup appointments THE SAME DAY (AM or PM) if MD is messaged and approves.

                 New visits:

  • Avoid booking last minute same day VCON appointments the morning of.

  • Okay to book same day VCON the day of if in the afternoon unit. MA’s to staff message MD via Teams as a courtesy.

What if I have trouble finding followup slots?

  • You can consider converting a TA30 to two TA15’s.

  • If you designate a patient as needing “urgent” followup (within 1-2 weeks) and there are no appointments available with you, the MA’s will let you know. You can then reach out to Ahilan to see if extra conversions are okay.

  • If you come across a patient requesting followup to discuss results/ questions and you have no appointments available, consider utilizing a PA to help communicate with patient if appropriate.

  1. Ask MA’s to call patients to convey a MD message.

    • Ex: remind patient they are due for followup labs, are overdue for scheduling an Ultrasound, were diagnosed with H. pylori and need to pick up prescribed triple therapy, have an unread secure message send by their doctor, etc. As a rule of thumb, anything that requires medical interpretation should not be sent to the MA’s. However, they can convey any message you ask them to.

  2. Answer any patient emails regarding procedure scheduling logistics, colonoscopy prep instructions/ diet questions. They can call the patient back and review.

  3. Questions about filling out paperwork such as FMLA or jury duty requests. They can advise patients to contact ROMI to assist.

  4. You can generate a Letter to the patient, then send a message to the MA to have them print and mail the letter to the patient.

  1. Result Note letters

  • For colonoscopy pathology, you can send the results to the MA’s with the simple phrase “Colon polyp path benign. Repeat colonoscopy advised in X years.” Most MDs have created QuikActions to do this for 3, 5, and 10 year surveillance intervals.

  • How to create a QuikAction:

    • To do this highlight the colonoscopy path result.

    • To the right, you will see a menu bar with a lightening bolt and the work “QuikAction”next to it. Clink on this, scroll to “Manage QuikActions”

  • On top left click “New QuikAction” (next to green plus sign), then “Result Note”

  • Fill in blanks- give your QuikAction a name (Ex: CRC 3 years)

  • Orders to Select: “All”

  • Route note to: “P (DRV or WCR) GI MA”

  • Under Result note, type what you’d like it to say. Example: “Colon polyp path benign. Repeat colonoscopy advised in X years.”

  • You can repeat this process for any benign colo path result and insert different surveillance intervals.

  • MA’s can send normal result letter for EGD, TURN, and normal labs.

  • You can also input whatever message you want to say to the patient de novo via “Result Note”, send to the MA’s, and they will cut and paste it into a letter and mail this to patient.

  1. MA Expectations for virtual clinic

  • MA’s are expected to check-in with clinic MD via Microsoft Teams by 8:45am and 1:45pm. Feel free to staff message them if you are running late, so they are aware and can plan to room your patient accordingly.

  • Perform medication reconciliation and input MDP diagnoses as part of rooming process.

  • Obtain a language line interpreter for VAV’s as needed.

  • In WCR – clear staff messages from podlet MD’s in real time.

  • In DRV- clear staff messages from all DRV MD’s in real time during the assigned clinic unit.

  • If you have urgent scheduling needs, feel free to reach out to the clinic MA via Teams, including scheduling of same day clinic followup appts.
