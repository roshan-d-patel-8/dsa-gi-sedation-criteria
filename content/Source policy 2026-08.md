# GI Anesthesia Procedure Criteria

This file preserves the policy text supplied for implementation. The application is a structured rendering and decision-support layer; clinical changes must be ratified against this source before release.

## Optiflow Criteria

- EGDs and TURNS BMI 43–50. Note in booking instruction on e-consult/case request. Need to get Optiflow case request order set.

## Criteria for MAC

- BMI >50 for EGD; BMI >50 for colonoscopy.
- Severe OSA (CPAP/BiPAP dependent) for EGD; conscious sedation for colonoscopy.
- Chronic pain with high-dose narcotic dependence:
  - MS Contin
  - Oral Dilaudid
  - Fentanyl patch
  - Opiate and benzodiazepine use
  - Norco, Percocet, or Vicodin >4 tablets/day
- Opioid antagonist use: naloxone, naltrexone, or buprenorphine/Suboxone.
- Heavy alcohol or drug use.
- Excessive pain/anxiety or prior intolerance to moderate sedation.
- History of sexual/physical trauma.
- ESRD on HD: arrive 2 hours early for potassium check.
- ESRD on peritoneal dialysis: arrive 2 hours early for antibiotics and potassium check.

## Criteria for MAC with POM clinic

- Symptomatic chronic lung disease.
- Symptomatic valvular disease.
- CHF with EF <30%.
- CAD with active stable angina.
- Oxygen dependence for colonoscopy. POM clinic may not be warranted if intermittent or low-dose oxygen (<2 L) and no active CHF flare.
- Intellectual disability/dementia but can tolerate pre-procedure IV.

Use best judgment. A patient with more than one of these issues may be a better candidate for OR, such as CHF with EF >30% and ESRD on HD. Seek anesthesia input when needed.

## Criteria for OR

- BMI >55 for EGD; BMI >60 for colonoscopy.
- Oxygen dependence for EGD.
- Home oxygen ≥4 L for colonoscopy.
- Severe gastroparesis.
- Intellectual disability/dementia but cannot tolerate pre-procedure IV.
- Severe pulmonary hypertension.
- Physical limitation such as quadriplegia/bedbound requiring multiple people to move.

All OR procedures should have POM clinic beforehand.

## POM guidelines for naltrexone and Contrave

- Naltrexone–bupropion (Contrave): discontinue 7 days before surgery and/or opioid administration.
- Naltrexone (Revia): stop 3 days before surgery.
- Long-acting IM naltrexone (Vivitrol): stop 1 month before surgery.

## Pleasanton exclusion criteria

1. BMI >42 for EGD or >50 for colonoscopy.
2. Home oxygen therapy.
3. Age >75.
4. Dialysis or renal failure.
5. High-risk intervention, including banding or EMR/removal of large polyps.
6. Severe sleep apnea requiring CPAP/BiPAP.
7. Known reversible coronary artery disease, including reversible defects or unstable angina.
8. Severe or poorly controlled congestive heart failure.
9. Deep brain stimulator.
10. Coagulopathy or thrombocytopenia with possible need for perioperative blood products.
11. Cirrhosis with MELD score ≥11.

## Remimazolam sedation criteria

- High-dose or long-acting opiates. Norco, Percocet, and Vicodin <4 tablets/day generally remain appropriate for moderate sedation.
- Failed moderate sedation in the past.
- Very heavy alcohol or drug use.
- No BMI criteria currently.
- No cardiopulmonary criteria currently.

Consider MAC for significant pain during colonoscopy with moderate sedation because remimazolam has no analgesic properties, and for heavy cannabis use.
